# Changelog

## 1.4.0
- Optional weather panel: date and condition on the left, condition icon
  in the centre overlapping the clock, current temperature and today's
  high/low on the right
- Location chosen by city search; data from Open-Meteo, no API key needed
- Celsius or Fahrenheit
- Weather icon animates on hover, with motion matching the condition:
  the sun rotates, clouds drift, rain and snow fall, storms flash, fog
  breathes. Nothing animates until the pointer is over the panel
- Weather hides automatically in a panel, like the date line

## 1.0.0
- Initial release
- Split-flap flip animation with a two-phase leaf rotation
- Dark, light, and follow-colour-scheme themes
- Seconds as a sweeping bar, a third flip card, or hidden
- 12/24 hour, optional date line
- Panel support: sizes to panel thickness in horizontal panels, stacks the
  cards in vertical ones
- Scales continuously with the widget
- Configurable font family
- Tooltip, and accessibility metadata for screen readers
